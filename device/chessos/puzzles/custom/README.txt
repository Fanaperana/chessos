Put your own puzzle .pgn files in this folder. They show up in Practice.

Each puzzle is a normal PGN game with a FEN header. The side to move in the
FEN is the side that solves the puzzle, and the moves are the solution
(opponent replies included):

[Event "My puzzle"]
[FEN "6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/5PPP/3R2K1 w - - 0 1"]
[SetUp "1"]

1. Rd8# *

Lichess-style puzzles, where the first move is the opponent's move that sets
up the puzzle, add the header [Premove "1"].

Optional headers: [Rating "1200"]  [Themes "fork short"]
